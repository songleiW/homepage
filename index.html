Biography

I am currently an Assistant Professor under the Hundred Talents Program at the School of Artificial Intelligence and the National Engineering Laboratory for Big Data System Computing Technology, Shenzhen University.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, under the supervision of Xiaohua Jia and Yifeng Zheng . Prior to that, I received my master's degree in Computer Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, under the supervision of Xiaohua Jia and Jiangang Shu , and my bachelor's degree in Internet of Things from China University of Petroleum (East China).

My research focuses on security and privacy, particularly secure multi-party computation and privacy-preserving data analytics. I am interested in developing efficient and secure solutions for practical applications, including privacy-preserving graph analytics, encrypted data processing, and privacy-preserving machine learning.
